How it works

From a photo to your page

  1. A real photograph

    We start from a public-domain wildlife photo where the animal fills the frame and its eye is clear.

  2. Matched to your pencils

    Our software maps every patch of feather or fur to the closest of the 24 standard pencils, then we touch up the eye and details by hand.

  3. You color, cell by cell

    Find the number, pick up that pencil, fill the cell. Light pressure lets the grid fade; layer two pencils to blend.

  4. Check with the key

    Compare your page with the free full-color answer key. Most plates take 2 to 4 relaxed hours.
